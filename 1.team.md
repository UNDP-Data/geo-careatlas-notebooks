# Team

Care Atlas is a joint initiative by the UNDP Gender Team and the Digital, AI and Innovation Hub. Each analysis is developed in partnership with the national and local institutions that use it, including ministries, municipalities, national statistics offices and disaster risk management agencies.

---

### Gender Team

UNDP works across some 170 countries and territories to advance gender equality, in partnership with governments, civil society, the private sector and academia. The Gender Team of UNDP's Global Policy Network supports this work globally and the implementation of UNDP's Gender Equality Strategy, working through regional and country teams.

Two regional teams currently work with the [Care Georeferencing Tool](/care_tool):

- **Latin America and the Caribbean.** The Regional Gender Team, based at the UNDP Regional Hub in Panama, designed and leads the CGT. Since 2020, it has developed and refined the methodology through country implementations, working with Country Offices and government partners to carry out the analyses and strengthen capacities to use, update and sustain them.
- **Asia and the Pacific.** The methodology is being adapted and implemented in the region by the Gender Equality Team at the UNDP Bangkok Regional Hub, working with Country Offices and government counterparts. 

---

### Digital, AI and Innovation Hub

The Digital, AI and Innovation Hub builds and maintains the Care Atlas platform, where CGT analyses can be published, explored and used directly in the browser. Geospatial data and layers are managed through [UNDP GeoHub](https://geohub.data.undp.org).
