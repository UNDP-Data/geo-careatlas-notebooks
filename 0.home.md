## <span style="color:#0468b1"> Mapping care to build resilient, gender-responsive societies </span>

Care Atlas brings together analyses built with UNDP's **Care Georeferencing Tool (CGT)**. Each one is an interactive map that shows where people need care, which services exist and who can reach them, helping decision-makers analyse where to invest first. In some cases, the analyses also include other relevant information, such as climate and disaster risk, multidimensional poverty, and access to health and education. All Care Maps gathered on this platform open in the browser, and each one documents its data sources and methods.

**[Explore the analyses →](/notebooks)**


### Why does UNDP map care?

Demand for childcare, long-term care and disability support is growing as populations age and more women enter the labour market. Climate-related shocks add pressure on households that already carry most care responsibilities, much of which is unpaid and carried out by women.

Several countries have made progress in designing and implementing care policies, but many still lack the integrated, gender-responsive territorial data needed to know where care needs are concentrated, how supply is distributed and where the most critical gaps lie.

To help close this gap, UNDP LAC's Gender Team developed the **Care Georeferencing Tool (CGT)**, which shows where care is needed, what services exist and who can reach them. **[Learn how the CGT works →](/care_tool)**

### About Care Atlas

Care Atlas is a joint initiative by the Gender Team and the Digital, AI and Innovation Hub.

Some partners host their Care Map on their own servers and connect it to their information systems. Care Atlas offers another way in: UNDP hosts the analysis, so partners can start using the results and bringing them into policy discussions without setting up their own IT infrastructure.

How to navigate it:

- **Explore** opens a region and its analyses.
- **Launch** opens an analysis as an interactive application. You do not need to read any code.
- **Edit** lets signed-in team members update and publish analyses.
- Every analysis states its data sources, date, methodology and main limitations. Results are estimates intended to support planning and policy dialogue.
